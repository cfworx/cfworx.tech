A structured seven-step loop applying to everything in the value system: products, services, practices, relationships, at every level.

| Step | Question | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What is the vision? | tie the improvement to organizational vision and strategy |
| 2 | Where are we now? | baseline the current state: metrics, stakeholder stories, feedback |
| 3 | Where do we want to be? | target state via SMART objectives, CSFs, KPIs |
| 4 | How do we get there? | plan the route, design the initiatives or experiments |
| 5 | Take action | execute, agile style: experiment, iterate, pivot |
| 6 | Are we getting there? | evaluate against baseline and target |
| 7 | How do we keep the momentum going? | embed the change into culture, plan the next loop |

- SMART = Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound.
- A failed initiative isn't wasted: analyze and document the lessons learned.
- Continual improvement is also one of the five [value system](/notes/it-service-management/itil-5-foundation/itil-value-system-and-governance/) components, so expect it from both angles.
