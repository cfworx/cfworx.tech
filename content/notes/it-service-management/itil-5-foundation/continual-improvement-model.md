A structured seven-step loop applying to everything in the value
system: products, services, practices, relationships, at every level.

1. **What is the vision?** Tie the improvement to organizational
   vision and strategy.
2. **Where are we now?** Baseline the current state: metrics,
   stakeholder stories, feedback.
3. **Where do we want to be?** Set the target state via SMART
   objectives, CSFs, KPIs.
4. **How do we get there?** Plan the route, design the initiatives or
   experiments.
5. **Take action.** Execute, agile style: experiment, iterate, pivot.
6. **Are we getting there?** Evaluate against the baseline and
   target.
7. **How do we keep the momentum going?** Embed the change into
   culture, plan the next loop.

SMART is Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound.

A failed initiative isn't wasted: analyze and document the lessons
learned.

Continual improvement is also one of the five
[value system](/notes/it-service-management/itil-5-foundation/itil-value-system-and-governance/)
components, so expect it from both angles.
