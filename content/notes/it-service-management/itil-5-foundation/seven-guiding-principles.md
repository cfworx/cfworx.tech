Universal recommendations that hold regardless of strategy or
structure. There's no hierarchy; use them together.

- **Focus on value**: every activity links to stakeholder value. The
  gotcha: first identify *who* you serve (customer, sponsor, user).
- **Start where you are**: reuse existing capabilities, tools,
  people. Starting from scratch wastes existing competence.
- **Progress iteratively with feedback**: small increments, MVPs,
  feedback loops. Avoid analysis paralysis; fail early.
- **Collaborate and promote visibility**: work across boundaries,
  make work visible. Collaboration is *not* consensus, and invisible
  work looks low priority.
- **Think and work holistically**: nothing stands alone; all four
  dimensions, end to end. Factor PESTLE into decisions.
- **Keep it simple and practical**: minimum steps, cut non-value
  work. Don't design for every exception; make general rules.
- **Optimize and automate**: tech scales the work *after* the process
  is sound. The sequence: understand, simplify, optimize, *then*
  automate. Never automate a bad process.

The optimize-and-automate ordering and the "collaboration is not
consensus" line are the two most quotable traps here.
