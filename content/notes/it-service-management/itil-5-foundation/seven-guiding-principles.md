Universal recommendations that hold regardless of strategy or structure. No hierarchy; use them together.

| Principle | Core idea | Gotcha |
|---|---|---|
| Focus on value | every activity links to stakeholder value | first identify WHO you serve (customer, sponsor, user) |
| Start where you are | reuse existing capabilities, tools, people | starting from scratch wastes existing competence |
| Progress iteratively with feedback | small increments, MVPs, feedback loops | avoid analysis paralysis; fail early |
| Collaborate and promote visibility | work across boundaries, make work visible | collaboration is NOT consensus; invisible work looks low priority |
| Think and work holistically | nothing stands alone; all four dimensions end to end | factor PESTLE into decisions |
| Keep it simple and practical | minimum steps, cut non-value work | don't design for every exception; make general rules |
| Optimize and automate | tech scales the work AFTER the process is sound | sequence: understand → simplify → optimize → THEN automate. Never automate a bad process |

The optimize-and-automate ordering and the "collaboration is not consensus" line are the two most quotable traps here.
