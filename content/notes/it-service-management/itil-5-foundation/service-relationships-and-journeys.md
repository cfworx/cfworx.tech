Service relationships are co-dependencies where organizations
co-create value.

## Roles

The service provider delivers and supports services. The service
consumer procures and uses them. The digital product vendor creates
and continually improves digital products and offerings.

The consumer sub-roles, the classic memorize-this set:

- **Customer**: defines requirements, owns the outcomes. The CIO
  negotiating the phone contract.
- **Sponsor**: authorizes the budget. The CFO approving the cost.
- **User**: uses the service daily. The employees on the phones.

## Relationship types

- **Basic**: support and efficiency, at the operational level.
  Commodity services, standard SLAs.
- **Cooperative**: improvement and effectiveness, operational plus
  tactical. Customized services, advanced SLAs.
- **Collaborative (partnership)**: innovation and growth, at all
  three levels. Bespoke services and contracts.

## The service journey

Seven stepping stones, iterative rather than sequential: Explore
(understand markets and needs), Engage (build the relationship, a
prerequisite for the closer types), Offer (shape demand and supply
into offerings), Agree (scope, quality, SLAs), Onboard (integrate
resources; offboard reverses it), Co-create (actually deliver and
consume), Reflect (evaluate value, feed improvements).

The band of visibility is how much of each organization the other can
see. The gotcha: the closer the relationship, the *wider* the band.
