Service relationships are co-dependencies where organizations co-create value.

## Roles

- Service provider delivers and supports services. Service consumer procures and uses them. Digital product vendor creates and continually improves digital products and offerings.
- Consumer sub-roles, the classic memorize-this table:

| Role | Owns | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Customer | defines requirements, owns the outcomes | CIO negotiating the phone contract |
| Sponsor | authorizes the budget | CFO approving the cost |
| User | uses the service daily | employees on the phones |

## Relationship types

| Type | Focus | Level | Flavor |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | support, efficiency | operational | commodity services, standard SLAs |
| Cooperative | improvement, effectiveness | operational + tactical | customized services, advanced SLAs |
| Collaborative (partnership) | innovation, growth | all three levels | bespoke services and contracts |

## The service journey

Seven stepping stones, iterative rather than sequential: Explore (understand markets and needs) → Engage (build the relationship; prerequisite for the closer types) → Offer (shape demand and supply into offerings) → Agree (scope, quality, SLAs) → Onboard (integrate resources; offboard reverses it) → Co-create (actually deliver and consume) → Reflect (evaluate value, feed improvements).

- Band of visibility: how much of each organization the other can see. Gotcha: the closer the relationship, the WIDER the band.
