## The four dimensions

Every one of them matters for co-creating value; ignore one and you build isolated, non-functional solutions.

| Dimension | Covers | Modern angle |
|---|---|---|
| Organizations and people | culture, roles, competencies, leadership | AI treated as a collaborative partner freeing humans for creative work |
| Information and technology | workflow systems, knowledge bases, analytics, cloud, data | out-of-context information has no intrinsic value; it becomes knowledge only when it enables outcomes |
| Partners and suppliers | service networks, contracts, supply chains | supplier strategy driven by core-vs-non-core focus, culture, resource scarcity, cost |
| Value streams and processes | workflows, controls, procedures, mapping | optimize flow; analyze cycle and wait time to cut waste |

## PESTLE

External factors beyond your control pressing on all four dimensions: Political (policy, stability), Economic (growth, funding, inflation), Social (workforce and consumer expectations), Technological (AI, disruption, automation), Legal (GDPR, compliance, IP), Environmental (sustainability rules, carbon targets).

## Complementary frameworks

| Framework | Fit with ITIL 5 | Trigger terms |
|---|---|---|
| Lean | flow efficiency, waste elimination in value streams | flow efficiency = cycle time as a share of total lead time |
| Agile | iterative increments and fast feedback | MVP for early feedback and pivots |
| DevOps | dev + ops collaboration, automated pipelines | CI/CD |
| PRINCE2 | temporary project structures aligned to strategy | PPM: portfolio, programme, project management |
