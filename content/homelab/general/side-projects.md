## Inland Care Jobs

[Inland Care Jobs](https://inlandcarejobs.com/) is a free health care
job board for the Inland Northwest:
[health care jobs in Spokane](https://inlandcarejobs.com/health-care-jobs-spokane-wa.html),
[Coeur d'Alene](https://inlandcarejobs.com/health-care-jobs-coeur-dalene-id.html),
and the smaller towns around them in eastern Washington and North
Idaho. Caregiver, CNA, and med tech jobs up through LPNs, RNs,
therapy, and providers.

- No account, and always free for job seekers.
- Listings come from employers' own public career sites: around 1,460
  openings from 71 employers across 44 towns as of October 2026.
- Filters for town, role, starting pay, shift, sign-on bonuses, and
  jobs that provide training.
- A directory of local training programs, like
  [CNA classes in Spokane and eastern Washington](https://inlandcarejobs.com/cna-classes-washington.html),
  with each program's length, cost, and format.
- Weekly email job alerts, typical posted pay by role, and local job
  fairs.

## Resurgence Builds

[Resurgence Builds](https://resurgencebuilds.com/) is an independent
fan site for The Division Resurgence, Ubisoft's free-to-play shooter
for mobile and PC (not affiliated with Ubisoft).

- [Division Resurgence build tier list](https://resurgencebuilds.com/tier-list/):
  the best builds, ranked by tier.
- [SHD Build Lab](https://resurgencebuilds.com/builder/): a loadout
  planner. Pick a specialization, weapons, gear sets, an OS Protocol,
  and skill mods, and it checks set bonuses, synergies, and conflicts.
  Builds share as a link.
- [Interactive Manhattan map](https://resurgencebuilds.com/division-resurgence-interactive-map/):
  safe houses and other points of interest, with progress saved in
  the browser.
- Guides, patch notes, and a database of exotic weapons, weapon
  talents, gear set effects, and drop rates.
